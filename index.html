About SmartMealPlanner

SmartMealPlanner is a WhatsApp-based assistant that helps users discover healthy and balanced meals every day. Users can interact directly through WhatsApp to receive personalized meal suggestions based on dietary preferences and nutrition goals.

Features

Daily Meal Plans

Get breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack recommendations instantly.

Healthy Diet Goals

Supports high protein, high fiber, vegetarian and non-vegetarian meal plans.

Quick Recipes

Receive easy recipes and cooking suggestions for each meal.

WhatsApp Convenience

No app download required. Simply message on WhatsApp to start.

How It Works

  1. Send a message to SmartMealPlanner on WhatsApp.
  2. Select your diet preference.
  3. Receive personalized meal suggestions.
  4. Request new meal plans anytime.

Privacy Policy

SmartMealPlanner respects user privacy. The service only uses the WhatsApp phone number to respond to meal planning requests initiated by the user.

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties. All conversations are used solely to provide the requested meal planning service.
