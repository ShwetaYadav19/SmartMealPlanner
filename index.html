SmartMealPlanner

SmartMealPlanner is a WhatsApp-based meal planning assistant that helps users discover healthy meals based on dietary goals such as high protein, high fiber, vegetarian, or non-vegetarian preferences.

Users can interact with SmartMealPlanner directly through WhatsApp to receive daily meal suggestions including breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.

How It Works

  • Users voluntarily send a message to the SmartMealPlanner WhatsApp number.
  • The bot responds with meal recommendations based on selected preferences.
  • Users can request new meal plans anytime.
